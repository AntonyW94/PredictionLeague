# 0013. No pay-to-win monetisation

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-05-30
- **Deciders:** Antony
- **Tags:** product

## Context

Some monetisation ideas (e.g. selling extra Boosts, or "insights" that improve predictions) could let players who spend more gain a competitive edge. The game's integrity depends on fairness.

## Decision

We will **not sell anything that confers a competitive advantage**. No purchasable boosts/power-ups. Paid features must be **convenience or cosmetic** only (e.g. SMS reminders, badges) and must never affect scoring or prediction outcomes.

## Consequences

**For / positive**
- Protects competitive integrity and player trust.
- Keeps monetisation clearly on the "service/cosmetic" side, reinforcing the non-gambling framing.

**Against / cost**
- Forgoes a potentially lucrative microtransaction line (paid boosts).

**Neutral / notes**
- "Insights" deferred; if introduced, kept to historical/analytical (review) content, not future-prediction edges.
- Season Challenges (0015) must be cosmetic/achievement only.
- SMS reminders are allowed because they're a convenience nudge, not an advantage; they're also additive (0009).

## Alternatives considered

- **Paid boosts / extra power-ups** — rejected; pay-to-win.
- **Predictive "insights" upsell** — deferred and constrained to non-advantage content.
